# Review: Leader Topic avatar identity

## Decision

Approved. Reuse the existing AgentMux green low-poly dragon asset for Leader Topic. Render it inside a visibly bounded rounded-rectangle badge in floating mode and the same smaller badge in compact mode. Keep status attention as a separate badge.

## Constraints

- Do not generate or keep a second character avatar for this product identity.
- Preserve the dragon asset's aspect ratio and recognizable silhouette.
- The floating badge remains a keyboard-focusable, clickable control.
- The compact badge is the same identity at a smaller size, not a different icon.
