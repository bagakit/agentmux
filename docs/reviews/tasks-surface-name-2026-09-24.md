Status: approved as a delegated product naming decision. The user requested a name for the Board surface from the user's point of view; Tasks is the chosen visible name.

## Accepted closure

- The global demand workspace is called Tasks in the main switch, breadcrumb, and primary toolbar title.
- Board remains an internal layout/surface identifier; Demand remains the persisted work item and control protocol identity.
- Renaming the surface does not reset selection, navigation, or recovery.

## Naming reason

Users come here to create and advance a piece of work. Board names one presentation shape. Tasks names the thing they seek. Mature work tools likewise name the primary object in navigation and offer boards as a view; see Linear's Issues and Projects model.
